01 / Master logo

One primary lockup. Use it first, always.

04 / Clear space

X equals the height of the mark

Keep a margin of at least ½ X clear on all four sides of the lockup — no type, rules, photography, buttons or page edges inside it. Where the layout allows, give it a full X and let the logo breathe.

05 / Minimum size

Below these sizes, drop the tagline — then the wordmark

The tagline is the first thing to fail as the lockup shrinks. Shown at true screen size.

320 px — full lockup

Tagline legible. Minimum for the tagline variant: 280 px digital, 70 mm print.

180 px — too small

Tagline breaks down. Use a lockup without the tagline, or the mark.

Mark: 48 / 32 / 24 / 16 px

Absolute floor 16 px, and only as a favicon. Anywhere else, 24 px.

09 / Color

One blue, one ink, two grounds

Brand blue is sampled straight from the logo file. The ramp exists for interface states — hovers, pressed states, tints and text on tinted fills — not for decoration. Brand blue on white is 3.6:1: fine for large type, icons and chrome, not for body copy. Use Blue 700 for paragraph-size blue text.

10 / Type & tagline

Archivo for everything the logo doesn't say

The wordmark and tagline inside the logo are artwork, not live text — never re-type them. Everywhere else, Lavelle sets in Archivo: 800 for headings, 600 for labels, 400 for body, flush left, tight tracking on display sizes.

Display / 800 / -0.03em
Accelerate networks
Heading / 800 / -0.02em

Managed SD-WAN
Body / 400 / 1.55
Branch, cloud and data-centre networks converge on one fabric. Body copy sets in ink at 15–17 px, flush left, never justified and never centered.
The tagline
Accelerate Networks for the Digital Economy

Title case, italic, always as supplied inside the logo artwork. If you need it as live text — a subject line, a slide subtitle — set it in Archivo 800 italic, sentence spacing unchanged. Never translate, shorten or punctuate it.

11 / Co-branding

Partner lockups sit behind a rule

Lavelle leads on the left. Separate the marks with a 2 px ink rule set to the height of the mark, and give each side at least ½X. Never merge, overlap or place a partner mark inside the blue field.
